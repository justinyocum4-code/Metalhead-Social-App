Privacy Policy – Metalhead Social

Last updated: September 17, 2026

What we collect

When you sign in to Metalhead Social with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from that provider. Inside the app we store the posts, replies, likes, and profile details you create.

How we use it

Your information is used only to run the app: identifying your account, showing your profile to other members, and delivering app features such as notifications. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Data is stored securely with our backend provider (Supabase). Login is handled through Google and Facebook's official sign-in systems.

Data deletion

You can delete your account and all of your data at any time. To request deletion, email justinyocum4@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Metalhead Social account". Your profile, posts, replies, and likes will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
